# CoFID potassium and phosphorus proof of concept

Retrieved **19 October 2026**. Read-only research; preliminary matching judgements, not clinical confidence scores or validated product measurements. **Four of 20 potential estimates are suitable for review: two potassium and two phosphorus estimates.** No application or database changes were made.

## Method and results

Verified each selected barcode directly through the [Open Food Facts (OFF) product API](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/api/v2/product/5000159553834.json). Barcode links below open the corresponding source records. Where the request did not specify a brand or pack, the selected UK-market variant is explicitly named. No different product was substituted for Ripple. RenalPlan's existing `public.cofid_foods` was queried with SELECT statements, retrieving food codes, descriptions, K/P and comparison nutrients. OFF country tags establish catalogue coverage, not independent verification of the current UK pack.

Numbers in the estimate columns are **CoFID database values proposed as estimates for a different, branded product**, not measured values for that barcode. Only “Suitable for review” candidates receive numbers. “Not available” means the evidence did not support an estimate; it does not mean zero. CoFID candidate values for withheld matches are recorded below for traceability, not recommended for use.

## Product comparisons and reasons

For compactness, OFF nutrition profiles below use **kcal; fat / saturates / carbohydrate / sugars / fibre / protein / salt**, with the latter seven in grams. These are the API's reported `_100g` fields, rounded here for readability, **not an independent confirmation of mass versus volume basis**. A dash means unavailable. No missing nutrient was converted to zero. Source K/P fields are absent except the explicitly discussed phosphorus zeros. OFF's separate `nutriments_estimated` object was excluded throughout.

1. **Ripple — unsuitable for both.** OFF profile: **526.7; 29 / 17.33 / 58 / 56.67 / — / 7.5 / 0.333**. The ingredient transcription mentions sugar, skimmed-milk material, whey permeate, palm/milk fats and an incomplete soya emulsifier description, but omits cocoa ingredients. OFF actually reports **P = 0g/100g** among numerous mineral zeros; K is absent. That zero is source-reported, not missing and not verified as genuine. Generic milk chocolate 17-648 reports K **451**, P **224** mg/100g, from 11 products; its 519 kcal, 31.1g fat and 7.3g protein resemble the macros, but cannot resolve the ingredient defect or validate the zero. Reject use of that generic entry for this record until the pack is checked.

2. **Dairy Milk — uncertain for each nutrient.** Selected the explicitly UK/Ireland multipack rather than the US-tagged OFF bar `7622210160324`. Profile: **533.1; 30.15 / 17.65 / 55.15 / 55.15 / 2.21 / 7.35 / 0.221**. Ingredients identify milk, sugar, cocoa butter/mass, palm/shea fats, E442/E476 and flavouring. CoFID 17-648 has similar macros, but no brand-specific cocoa/milk proportions. OFF also reports a questionable cocoa field of **73.53g/100g**, requiring pack verification. K remains uncertain independently of P. E442 is ammonium phosphatides, a phosphorus-containing emulsifier; its quantity and representation in the CoFID composite are unknown. Do not conflate it with inorganic phosphate salts or infer its phosphorus contribution. Candidate K/P **451/224**, withheld. [E442 chemical specification](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32008L0084).

3. **Whole milk — uncertain for each nutrient.** Profile: **66; 3.7 / 2.4 / 4.7 / 4.7 / 0 / 3.5 / 0.1075**; ingredients say whole milk only. CoFID 12-596 reports **157/96** mg K/P, with 63 kcal, 3.6g fat and 3.4g protein; its description combines seasonal standardised-milk analytical and industry data. OFF does not establish pasteurisation versus another process, and liquid-label mass/volume basis is unresolved. It reports **P = 0**, multiple other mineral zeros and calcium **248mg** in its `_100g` field, which require checking against the original serving column. No phosphate additive is listed, but that does not validate P=0. An alternative Tesco record `5000119115874` had empty ingredients; it was not silently substituted. Both estimates withheld pending current pack/process/unit confirmation.

4. **Cheddar — suitable for review for each nutrient.** Profile: **416.7; 34.9 / 21.7 / 0.1 / 0.1 / — / 25.4 / 1.77**; calcium **739mg/100g**; ingredients identify Cheddar. This is a block of natural extra-mature cheese, not processed cheese spread or grated cheese with additives. CoFID 12-346 covers 20 English Cheddar samples, including mild/mature and seasonal samples: 416 kcal, fat 34.9g, protein 25.4g, carbohydrate 0.1g, salt 1.8075g, calcium 739mg. The [manufacturer's extra-mature page](https://www.cathedralcity.co.uk/en/our-cheese/block/extra-mature-cheddar) corroborates those comparison nutrients. No phosphate salt or fortification is declared in the retrieved ingredient record. K **75**, P **505** are plausible review candidates, not validated branded measurements; matching label macros do not independently validate minerals.

5. **Hovis — uncertain for each nutrient.** Profile: **220; 1.75 / 0.5 / 37.75 / 4 / 6.75 / 10 / 0.8875**. Ingredients include wholemeal wheat flour, water, added wheat protein, yeast, soya flour, salt, E282, caramelised sugar, E472e, ascorbic acid and fortified wheat flour with calcium, folic acid, iron, niacin and thiamin. Generic untoasted wholemeal 11-981 has 217 kcal, protein 9.4g, fat 2.5g and carbohydrate 42g, from 21 sliced/unsliced samples. Additional protein/soya, flour proportions and unspecified calcium form prevent a defensible mineral transfer. No explicit phosphate is listed; that alone cannot establish equivalence. Candidate K/P **253/202**, withheld. Toasted 11-982 is inappropriate because its concentration reflects calculated water loss.

6. **Corn Flakes — uncertain for each nutrient.** Profile: **378; 0.9 / 0.2 / 84 / 8 / 3 / 7 / 0.5**. Maize, barley malt extract, sugar and salt, with niacin, iron and several B vitamins/vitamin D. CoFID 11-742 is the correct dry fortified cereal type and includes Kellogg's among 15 samples/9 products: 376 kcal, fat 0.8g, protein 7.1g, carbohydrate 90.9g and salt 1.125g; candidate K/P **88/46**. Different carbohydrate methodology may explain part of the comparison, but the salt/fortification record needs pack verification. No phosphate additive appears in the transcription; mineral fortificant forms and current formulation are not established. Do not use unfortified, frosted or nut-coated entries to force a match.

7. **Heinz beans — suitable for review for each nutrient.** Profile: **79; 0.2 / 0.1 / 12.9 / 5 / 3.7 / 4.7 / 0.6**. Ingredients specify beans **50%**, tomatoes **36%**, water, sugar, modified cornflour, salt and spice/herb extracts; no phosphate additive or mineral fortification is listed. CoFID 13-532 is canned beans with tomato sauce, 16 samples/9 brands: 81 kcal, protein 5g, fat 0.5g, carbohydrate 15g, sugars 4.8g and salt 0.6525g. Same food/process and closely comparable profile support K **272**, P **88** for review, using beans **with sauce, not drained**. Reduced-salt/sugar 13-535 is explicitly estimated from 13-532/industry data and was not used; barbecue 13-578 has null K/P and cannot supply zeros.

8. **Walkers — uncertain for each nutrient.** Profile: **500; 27 / 2.7 / 56 / 0.8 / 0.9 / 8.3 / 1.18**. Potatoes, mixed sunflower/rapeseed oils, salt, rosemary extract, ascorbic acid, tocopherol-rich extract and citric acid; no explicit phosphate additive. CoFID 17-671 reports **1328/135** mg K/P, 493 kcal, fat 28.8g, protein 6.2g and carbohydrate 55.8g. Its 11 samples/8 products include Walkers but **assorted flavours**, fried in high-oleic sunflower oil. Neither potato composition/process variation for K nor flavouring-related mineral differences for P is resolved. Do not transfer the pooled mineral values to ready-salted solely because the brand appears in its description.

9. **Philadelphia — uncertain for each nutrient.** UK/Ireland record selected instead of French/Belgian `7622210955562`; pack size absent. Profile: **226.7; 20.67 / 13.67 / 4.33 / 4.33 / 0.33 / 5.33 / 0.7375**. Full-fat soft cheese, salt, guar gum and citric acid, made with pasteurised milk; no phosphate salt declared. CoFID 12-551 includes Philadelphia and own brands, 20 samples: 252 kcal, fat 24.4g, protein 5.3g and carbohydrate 3g; candidate K/P **89/97**. Fat and carbohydrate differences suggest that pooled older formulation/moisture values need review. Similar protein alone does not establish mineral equivalence. Light and extra-light entries were rejected as different products.

10. **Coca-Cola — K uncertain; P unsuitable for this transfer.** Profile: **42.4; 0 / 0 / 10.6 / 10.6 / — / 0 / 0**. Multinational OFF record has a Spanish ingredient transcription: carbonated water, sugar, caramel E150d, phosphoric acid and flavourings/caffeine. The [GB manufacturer](https://www.coca-cola.com/gb/en/brands/coca-cola-original-taste) confirms the ingredient type but labels nutrition **per 100ml**. No density conversion to 100g was established. Generic CoFID cola 17-175 covers 10 samples/6 brands: 41 kcal, carbohydrate/sugars 10.9g, K **1**, P **30mg/100g**. K cannot simply be presumed zero; mass/volume and market formulation remain uncertain. P transfer is rejected because the product-specific phosphoric-acid quantity is unknown: matching sugar cannot validate phosphorus. Diet cola 17-505 is calculated from cola, not independent evidence.

## Evidence limits and recommendation

**K: 2 suitable, 7 uncertain, 1 unsuitable. P: 2 suitable, 6 uncertain, 2 unsuitable. Total: 4 suitable, 13 uncertain, 3 unsuitable.** These are separate nutrient judgements, not confidence percentages. A rejected estimate is not evidence that the food is unsuitable to eat.

The [official CoFID publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/composition-of-foods-integrated-dataset-cofid) and [2021 user guide, pp. 4–8](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/971021/McCance_and_Widdowsons_Composition_of_Foods_integrated_dataset_2021.pdf) explain composite sampling, reformulation and typical rather than definitive composition. Values normally use 100g; `N` signifies insufficient reliable quantitative information and `Tr` a trace. RenalPlan's numeric columns do not retain all original cell qualifiers or nutrient-level analytical provenance. Reported CoFID numbers above are therefore **database-reported**, not asserted to be independently measured for every mineral. Descriptions explicitly identifying calculation or estimation were distinguished and not used as independent confirmation.

OFF search endpoints returned HTTP 503/temporary-unavailability HTML; public search located candidate barcodes, followed by successful direct API verification for all ten selected records. Browser search access to OFF itself was blocked, but direct API access worked. Product images/current physical labels were not independently checked. Retrieved values are a dated catalogue snapshot, not a guarantee of today's formulation. Discovery-only nonmatching US Cadbury, non-UK Philadelphia and missing Hovis half-loaf records were excluded. No barcodes or mineral quantities were invented.

**Recommendation:** do not build an automatic clinical-facing matching engine on this evidence. The four review candidates justify a small manually curated validation exercise. Before release, a renal dietitian/food-composition specialist should review each nutrient separately; confirm current UK barcode, ingredients, processing, fortification/additive forms and mass/volume basis against pack images/manufacturer data; recover original CoFID references/qualifiers; and compare proposed estimates with independent manufacturer mineral specifications or laboratory analyses across batches. Define acceptable nutrient-specific error and abstention rules, provenance/version tracking and reformulation checks before evaluating automation on a larger independent product set. Keep unsupported nutrients unavailable and estimates explicitly distinguished from reported measurements.

**Traceability:** all links and database rows were accessed on **19 October 2026**. OFF supplies the product descriptions, ingredient transcriptions and reported nutrition (ODbL); each table barcode links its record, whose JSON can be retrieved at `https://world.openfoodfacts.org/api/v2/product/{barcode}.json`. CoFID evidence is from RenalPlan project `yyxjeukraeibxxloaors`, `public.cofid_foods`, filtered by the exact food codes above. Only read-only SELECT queries and public HTTP GET requests were used. No second report, exports or dependencies were created; no commit, push or deployment was performed.
